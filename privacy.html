All Mail: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

Key Summary: All Mail is built with privacy at its core. We do NOT collect, read, store, or transmit your email login credentials, emails, or personal communications. All logins occur directly with official email providers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

When you log into your email accounts (such as Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Proton, iCloud, or others):

  • We do not have access to your passwords or two-factor authentication codes.
  • We do not read, scan, or process your email content, subject lines, or attachments.
  • All connections are made directly between your device and the respective email service via secure HTTPS using standard Apple WebKit containers.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following non-sensitive preferences are stored solely on your local device and never transmitted to our servers:

  • Your customized list of selected email providers on your home screen.
  • Your offline email templates and custom drafts.
  • Local mail follow-up reminders scheduled through iOS Notification Center.
  • Your preferred theme and appearance settings.

3. Biometric Security (Face ID / Touch ID)

All Mail provides an optional Biometric Lock feature to safeguard your session. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple's secure enclave (LocalAuthentication framework). We do not have access to your biometric data.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

For users on our free tier, the app may display advertisements via Google AdMob. These services may collect anonymous device identifiers in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework if consent is granted.

Users who subscribe to All Mail Pro enjoy a 100% ad-free experience, and no advertising tracking is performed.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All financial transactions and subscriptions are processed directly by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your credit card or billing details.
